Peace Rising

An alarm clock that wakes you with a word from God, chosen for how you feel.

  1. Set your alarm before bed, with a verse to sleep on.
  2. Wake to a gentle chime that grows slowly, with a sunrise on screen.
  3. Silence it by checking in. Say how you feel and answer two quick questions.
  4. Receive scripture matched to your morning, a short prayer and a moment to reflect.
  5. Pray together in Peace Rings, small groups of people who wake when you do. An account is only needed here.
Try it on your phone

Point your phone camera at the code to open Peace Rising. Add it to your home screen for the full-screen app.
